03 · AI Security · DevSecOps

ShipGuard AI — AI-Powered Security Triage

A static-analysis triage platform that turns raw Semgrep, ESLint, and gitleaks output into a small, prioritized set of findings — each explained against the real surrounding code by an LLM, cross-verified across models, and connected through a Neo4j knowledge graph so a change's blast radius is visible, not guessed.

Real-world problem: Static analyzers dump hundreds of raw findings with no sense of what actually matters. ShipGuard ranks and explains them, flags recently-changed code as higher risk, and surfaces the ones worth a human's time instead of a wall of noise.

Findings explained, ranked & graphed — not just listed
